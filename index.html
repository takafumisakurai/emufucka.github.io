EMUFUCKA

EMUFUCKA is a Tokyo-based electronic artist and producer working across future beats, bass, and live performance. A Red Bull Music Academy New York 2013 alumnus, he has released solo and collaborative work and performed at SXSW, Sónar, Ultra Japan, The Do-Over, and independent venues worldwide.
