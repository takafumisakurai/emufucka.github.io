Short bio

EMUFUCKA is a Tokyo-based artist and producer whose sound moves between intricate beat programming, melodic electronics, bass, and live improvisation.

A Red Bull Music Academy New York 2013 alumnus, he has released solo records and remixes, collaborated with artists across the global beat scene, and performed in Japan, Europe, and the United States.

Extended bio — adapted from the Red Bull Music Academy 2013 artist profile.

EMUFUCKA builds bright, intricately programmed rhythms that leave room for improvisation and flashes of playfulness.

His collaborators include Submerse, Djemba Djemba, DZA, Quarta330, Coco Bryce, King Henry of Virtual Boy, My Dry Wet Mess, and Tokyo Hands. He has appeared alongside Rustie, Ben UFO, Shlohmo, and TOKiMONSTA; was selected for Red Bull Music Academy New York in 2013; and released singles and EPs through labels including Black Hole Recordings and Fremdtunes.

His live appearances include Sónar in Spain, Ultra Japan, SXSW in the United States, V-ROX in Russia, The Do-Over in the United States, and Japan Future Now in France. His work has been covered by independent and mainstream music media.
